About Nomad.

Our story, mission and vision for safe, authentic travel across Egypt.

Our story

Born in Cairo, built for travelers

Nomad started with one frustration: Egypt is extraordinary, but planning a trip here felt like hard work — scattered prices, unknown guides, endless tabs. So we built a single sunlit platform where every price is transparent, every guide is verified, and every question gets a human answer.

Today Nomad connects thousands of travelers with licensed Egyptian experts — from Giza sunrise walks to Blue Hole dives and Siwa salt floats — while keeping bookings secure and flexible.

🎯

Our Mission

Make Egypt the easiest extraordinary trip on Earth: honest pricing, verified local experts, and human support from first search to final sunset.

🔭

Our Vision

A tourism economy where every pound a traveler spends fairly reaches Egyptian communities — guides, drivers, boat crews and family kitchens.

🛡️

Safe & Authentic

Licensed guides only, secure sign-in bookings, free 48h cancellation, and a 24/7 hotline (19772) on every trip. No surprises, ever.

Core objectives

  • ✔ Verify every guide — license, reviews and real identity checked
  • ✔ Show full price breakdowns before anyone pays a pound
  • ✔ Keep humans in the loop — real chats, real negotiation, real help
  • ✔ Reward loyalty — points on every completed trip unlock discounts
  • ✔ Support local communities across all 10 featured destinations
