Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: September 2026 — how we protect your information.

Welcome to Nomad ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our platform, sign in via Google, or book a trip, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • ✔ Name and Email Address (via Google Authentication).
  • ✔ Profile Picture URL.
  • ✔ Trip preferences, saved custom itineraries, and booking history.
  • ✔ Feedback and reviews submitted on destinations or guides.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • ✔ Facilitate account creation and secure authentication via Google Sign-In.
  • ✔ Manage and confirm your travel bookings, group waiting lists, and custom programs.
  • ✔ Calculate loyalty points, discounts, and rewards.
  • ✔ Provide customer support, hotline services, and local SIM card concierge services.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please also remember that we cannot guarantee that the internet itself is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Identity Services for secure authentication. Google's collection and use of your data are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may contact our support team directly through the platform's customer support widget or hotline.
